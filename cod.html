Before you download

4K, 2K or 1080p?

Every scene comes in all three, with the same footage, framing and frame rate. Only the detail and the file size change.

On a computer

Take whichever you like. 4K keeps every detail; 2K and 1080p are quicker to download.

On a phone

Many phones can’t play or edit 4K at 60 fps. If a 4K file won’t open or import into your editing app, choose a lower resolution.

    Footage & audio details

    The same scenes in every quality, each file under 2 GB. Native 30/60 fps and cinematic framing are preserved. Most footage is 3840 × 1634 in 4K, 2560 × 1090 in 2K, or 1920 × 816 in 1080p.

    4K keeps the video from the existing size-capped pack without another video encode. 2K and 1080p are high-quality smaller conversions. All editions use H.264 video and one English AAC stereo track where recovered. Meltdown outro has no associated audio.

    The 1080p/60 Kill Archer Outro sample exported successfully on a Samsung A55, as confirmed by its user. Other files and the 2K/4K editions have not been tested on that phone. Successful export depends on your device and editing app. The footage is SDR.

    Campaign numbering follows the game's extracted progression data; runtime playback order was not observed in-game.
