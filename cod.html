      4K, 2K or 1080p?

      Every scene comes in all three, with the same footage, framing and frame rate. Only the detail and the file size change.

      On a computer

      Take whichever you like. 4K keeps every detail; 2K and 1080p are quicker to download.

      On a phone

      Many phones can’t play or edit 4K. If a 4K file won’t open or import into your editing app, choose a lower resolution.
