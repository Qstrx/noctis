# Character artwork sources

Character cards use game imagery. The images retain their original rights; the
project does not claim ownership of the characters or artwork.

## Corrected portraits

The Dunn and Ko Jin Hyuk cards use the **Modern Warfare 4 (2026)** characters.
They are not portraits of the actors, generic soldiers, or characters with the
same surname from another game.

| Card | Character | Image reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dunn | Joseph Dunn, portrayed by William Lipton | [Dexerto character table](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/modern-warfare-4/characters/), [game portrait](https://media.dexerto.com/media/dunn-modern-warfare-4.jpg) |
| Ko Jin Hyuk | Ko Jin Hyuk, portrayed by Lanny Joon | [Dexerto character table](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/modern-warfare-4/characters/), [game portrait](https://media.dexerto.com/media/ko-jin-hyuk-modern-warfare-4.jpg) |

Dunn's identity was also cross-checked against the labeled Campaign Operator
chart in the [official Call of Duty launch article](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-launch-multiplayer-operators-weapons-equipment-create-a-class).

Both portraits were inspected before conversion. Their faces and content are
unaltered; only WebP encoding, thumbnail resizing and the established blur for
locked sections were applied. The character's locked/unlocked availability is
separate from the artwork and is configured in the page data.

Machine-readable source URLs, retrieval date, processing notes, and SHA256
checksums are recorded in
[`assets/images/cover/sources.json`](../assets/images/cover/sources.json).
